# PickleIQ — Product Vision

## Elevator Pitch

PickleIQ is a free AI-powered web app that turns raw pickleball match videos into shareable highlight reels and personalized coaching reports — no coach required.

---

## Target Customers

### Individual Players
Recreational and competitive players who record their matches but lack the time or expertise to review footage objectively. They want to improve but don't have consistent access to coaching.

### Coaches
Coaches who spend hours manually reviewing student footage. PickleIQ automates the tedious parts — rally detection, clip extraction, and initial feedback — so coaches can focus on personalized instruction.

### Academies
Pickleball academies with multiple students across skill levels. PickleIQ provides a scalable way to deliver consistent, data-driven feedback without proportionally increasing coaching hours.

---

## Problem Statement

Players lack access to objective self-analysis of their match footage. Watching raw match video is time-consuming and unfocused. Without structured feedback, players repeat the same mistakes and struggle to identify real improvement areas. Hiring a coach for video review is expensive and not always available.

---

## Solution

Upload a match video. PickleIQ automatically:
1. Detects rally segments across the match
2. Generates a 60-second highlight reel of the best moments
3. Produces an AI coaching report with strengths, improvement areas, and drill recommendations

No account required. No coach needed. Results ready in minutes.

---

## Phase 1 Success Metrics

| Metric | Target |
|--------|--------|
| Time from upload to results | < 5 minutes |
| Rally detection coverage | > 80% of actual rallies identified |
| Highlight reel duration | 55–65 seconds |
| Coaching report sections | 4 (strengths, improvements, drills, summary) |
| Player satisfaction (informal) | "This is useful" from first 10 testers |

---

## What PickleIQ Is Not (Phase 1)

- Not a coaching platform with accounts or student management
- Not a ball-tracking or shot-classification tool
- Not a subscription product
- Not a mobile app
